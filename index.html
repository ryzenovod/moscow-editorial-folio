About Egor Belousov

23 works exploring the rhythm of late October in Moscow

This editorial collection captures the distinct atmosphere of Moscow in autumn: tailored wool overcoats against neoclassical facades, golden foliage in Alexander Garden, quiet contemplation on the metro, and spontaneous moments of friendship.

Expanded with custom master editorial magazine covers, Swiss typographic exhibition posters, and tactile film contact sheets, shot with an emphasis on authentic texture and classic Leica M11 and Kodak Portra 400 optics.

What equipment and aesthetic defines this series?

The series draws inspiration from 35mm and medium-format rangefinders (Leica M11 Monochrom, Hasselblad) paired with prime 50mm and 85mm lenses at wide apertures for creamy subject separation.

Can these images be licensed for editorial print?

Yes, all 20 frames are available in master 2K/4K resolution for editorial, magazine, and portfolio publication.
